# Research and grounding

Audience research gives a synthetic panel an explicit evidence basis. It does not turn the panel into a human sample or prove that its modeled profiles represent population prevalence.

## What grounding does

Grounding helps the system:

- define the audience and exclusions precisely;
- keep materially different cohorts separate;
- identify supported roles, contexts, needs, objections, and decision patterns;
- distinguish observed, estimated, experimental, and unknown attributes;
- preserve evidence conflicts and missing support;
- create reusable profile snapshots without copying named people.

## Evidence lanes

Research stays separated by purpose.

### Structural evidence

Structural sources describe a possible population frame or allocation basis. Different universes—people, firms, employers, establishments, accounts, or transactions—cannot be merged into one denominator without evidence connecting them.

### Surveys and professional research

Recurring surveys, industry studies, and professional research can support broad patterns when their population, date, geography, method, and limitations match the question.

### Social, community, review, and forum evidence

These sources are useful for language, recurring concerns, workflows, and contextual hypotheses. Post volume and engagement are not audience prevalence.

### Approved aggregate first-party evidence

Privacy-reviewed aggregates can inform distributions, cohort context, and carefully bounded hypotheses. Raw rows never enter the panel-building workflow.

### Historical performance evidence

Historical results can provide context or motivate later validation. They do not establish prospective creative performance and must remain separate from held-out outcome evidence.

## From evidence to profiles

The builder links findings to source items, evaluates conflicts and sufficiency, then constructs only supported combinations of role, operating context, mindset, and buying situation. It avoids an automatic cross-product of every attribute.

Every explicit combination needs evidence or an `experimental` label. Missing information remains visible rather than being filled with generic persona detail.

## Approval is for meaningful disagreement

In a reusable research-backed build, approval gates let the user challenge:

- the audience decision and cohort boundaries;
- the research plan and important missing sources;
- the evidence synthesis and unresolved conflicts;
- the constructed panel and unsupported combinations;
- whether the exact immutable package should be registered for reuse.

The [provisional no-research route](../guides/build-an-audience-without-research.md) omits those research and package approvals because there is no research package to review.

## What research does not establish

Research-backed profiles do not establish:

- a representative human sample;
- human response rates or population shares;
- synthetic-replicate independence;
- future campaign performance;
- causal audience behavior.

Those limits are explained in [Synthetic evidence and validity](synthetic-evidence-and-validity.md).
