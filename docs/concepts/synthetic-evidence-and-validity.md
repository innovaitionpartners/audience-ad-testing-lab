# Synthetic evidence and validity

Ad Testing Lab produces model-conditional evidence: what happened when a frozen audience model, creative set, protocol, runtime, and statistical method were applied together.

## What it can support

Synthetic evidence can help:

- stress-test finished messages before spending media budget;
- reveal recurring objections, comprehension problems, and profile differences;
- compare creatives under one controlled protocol;
- identify stable candidates for a live test;
- preserve dissent and unresolved boundaries;
- generate hypotheses that real campaign evidence can later examine.

## What it cannot support

It cannot establish:

- population preference or prevalence;
- customer, consumer, or survey percentages;
- human-sample independence or statistical significance;
- predicted CTR, conversion, pipeline, revenue, or lift;
- causal effects;
- real-world validity of a panel without separate eligible evidence.

## Run validity states

### `incomplete`

Collection is still open or required records are missing. No completed conclusion should be presented.

### `invalid`

The design or result fails a structural requirement, such as disconnected comparisons or an unidentified model. The system refuses to interpret it.

### `exploratory`

The run is usable for directional learning but misses one or more calibrated recovery, coverage, stability, disagreement, or sensitivity requirements. It cannot manufacture a resolved shortlist.

### `valid`

The closed run passed every required gate under its frozen policy. This means valid under the protocol—not validated human-market behavior.

Boundary and finalist approval states remain separate from screening validity.

## Why refusal states matter

A trustworthy workflow must be able to say:

- the library needs to be split;
- more authorized capacity is required;
- a cutoff remains unresolved;
- the profile mix is distorted;
- the evidence is exploratory;
- no roster can be proposed.

Those are product outputs, not failures to “try hard enough.”

## Stability is conditional

Bootstrap resampling, usable-record floors, balanced profiles, connectivity checks, and sensitivity analyses test whether the synthetic result is recoverable within the frozen design. They do not estimate population uncertainty.

An honestly single-profile provisional scope can mark cross-profile sensitivity as not applicable, but its claims must remain conditional on that one modeled context.

## Human review remains necessary

People approve the frozen study plan and, later, the finalist roster. An override remains labeled. The model does not hide uncertainty behind a single trust score.
