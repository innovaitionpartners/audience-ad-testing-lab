The basic idea

This type of test creates synthetic panelists from evidence-grounded buyer profiles, shows them the ads, records their immediate reactions and comparisons, and looks for patterns that repeat inside the test. It helps a marketer decide which ideas deserve more attention and what to test next.

    What the results mean

    The rankings, themes, and closer-review choices describe this synthetic test only. They can guide a creative decision, but they are not customer survey results, market preference, or proof of campaign performance.
